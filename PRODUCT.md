# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Nuxt 3 (Vue 3) + TypeScript, Pinia, Tailwind CSS, GSAP, canvas-confetti

## Users

Gamers, students, and professionals seeking playful motivation for everyday learning tasks and skill development. They want to turn otherwise dry educational objectives, coursework, or self-study routines into engaging adventures.

## Product Purpose

QUEST is an educational adventure and interactive learning platform that transforms learning objectives, coursework, and habit building into a quest-driven journey. Success means learners feel intrinsically motivated, retain momentum through playful feedback loops, and conquer learning milestones like epic adventures.

## Positioning

An educational adventure experience powered by immersive gamification—translating real-world learning milestones and study tasks into character progression, XP gains, level-ups, streak bonuses, achievements, and milestone "boss battles" that cannot be matched by plain checklist apps or generic flashcard tools.

## Operating Context

- Browsers on desktop and mobile devices.
- Daily study sessions, learning sprints, coding/knowledge challenges, and review intervals.
- Integrated audio-visual cues (micro-animations, GSAP motion, celebration effects like canvas-confetti) that make task completions and level-ups feel rewarding.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Dynamic quest management (daily quests, epic milestone sagas, side quests).
- Progression mechanics: XP system, level progression, streaks, badges/achievements, and milestone boss encounters.
- Smooth animations and state transitions powered by GSAP and dynamic celebratory feedback (canvas-confetti).
- Nuxt 3 with Pinia for modular state management and TypeScript typing throughout.
- Responsive layout prioritizing clean, immersive game-like aesthetics with rapid load and seamless navigation.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone & Voice**: Motivating, playful, adventurous, clear, and empowering. Avoid cheesy or patronizing gaming cliches.
- **Visual Identity**: High craft, immersive yet scanable, premium dark/vibrant aesthetics with deliberate contrast, rich micro-interactions, and satisfying feedback.

## Product Principles

1. **Learning is the Hero's Journey**: Every module, study task, or practice session represents a tangible step in the character's adventure.
2. **Immediate Gratification Meets Deep Mastery**: Celebrate micro-wins instantly (XP, motion, sound/visual cues) while anchoring progress to authentic comprehension and skill mastery.
3. **Clarity Over Clutter**: Game mechanics amplify focus and motivation; they should never obscure task details, deadlines, or learning materials.
4. **Resilient Habit Loops**: Momentum and streaks motivate consistency, with forgiveness and recovery mechanics rather than punitive demotivation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Adhere to WCAG 2.1 AA standards for color contrast and legible typography.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for GSAP animations and celebratory confetti effects.
- Full keyboard navigability for quest interactions, task completion, and inventory/stats navigation.
